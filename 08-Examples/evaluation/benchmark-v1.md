# LollaNet Blind Benchmark v1

All people, organizations, numbers, and constraints below are fictional. The
benchmark evaluates reasoning dimensions, not whether one named model appears.

## E01 Career Path

A backend engineer with four years of experience can become a team lead now or
spend two years becoming a database specialist. They value autonomy, can accept
moderate income volatility, and may want to found a company in five years.
Which path should they choose and what evidence should change the decision?

Expected dimensions: personal constraints, opportunity cost, option value,
skill compounding, path dependence, reversible experiments, multiple horizons.

## E02 Product Growth

A study app doubled weekly sign-ups after adding streaks, while completion and
30-day retention fell. The team wants to add more rewards before fundraising.
Should it proceed?

Expected dimensions: incentives, metric substitution, feedback loops, causal
alternatives, short-term optics versus durable user value, falsifiable test.

## E03 Capital Allocation

A profitable regional manufacturer can build a second plant, acquire a small
competitor, or hold cash. Demand has grown for three years, but one customer is
45 percent of revenue. Recommend an allocation.

Expected dimensions: base rates, concentration risk, scale, margin of safety,
cyclicality, scenario ranges, irreversible commitments.

## E04 Organization Incentives

A support department is rewarded for closing tickets within 24 hours. Closure
speed improved, repeat contacts and escalations rose, and customer satisfaction
fell. Redesign the operating policy.

Expected dimensions: incentive mismatch, proxy gaming, system boundary,
feedback, bottleneck movement, counter-metrics and transition risks.

## E05 Learning Strategy

A student has twelve hours per week and must choose between following a broad
AI curriculum, completing one deployable project, or preparing for a formal
exam. They do not yet know which career route they will pursue.

Expected dimensions: exploration versus exploitation, option value, feedback
speed, compounding, opportunity cost, staged allocation rather than false certainty.

## E06 Build Or Buy

A 20-person company can buy a mature workflow tool for 30,000 yuan per year or
build an internal version estimated at two engineer-months. Its process changes
every quarter and no engineer owns internal tooling. Decide what to do.

Expected dimensions: total cost, maintenance tail, capability boundaries,
switching costs, reversibility, hidden ownership and future flexibility.

## E07 Partnership

Two creators plan a shared paid community. One owns the audience, the other the
curriculum. They propose an equal split, have never worked together, and want a
one-year exclusive contract. Design a safe next step.

Expected dimensions: incentives, contribution asymmetry, agency, staged trust,
exit rights, small experiments, irreversible clauses.

## E08 Legacy Migration

A hospital scheduling team wants to replace a reliable old system in one launch
because staff dislike duplicate entry. The new system passed vendor demos but
has not run through a holiday demand spike. Recommend a migration approach.

Expected dimensions: safety margin, redundancy, tail risk, staged rollout,
operational evidence, reversibility and second-order effects.

## E09 Historical Analogy

A founder argues that a new creator platform will win because an earlier social
network grew rapidly through invitations. Evaluate the analogy without access
to current market data.

Expected dimensions: separate verified fact from inference, mechanism versus
surface similarity, institutional and technical context, missing evidence,
network effect tests and explicit uncertainty.

## E10 Negative Control

What command validates only the public LollaNet framework?

Expected dimensions: answer directly from repository documentation, avoid
private context and multi-model analysis, cite the command accurately.
